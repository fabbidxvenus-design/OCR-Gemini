# Product Mission

## Problem

Warehouse workers currently spend too much time manually entering information from product labels and warehouse documents.

## Target Users

Primary users are warehouse workers who directly scan labels, review OCR output, and prepare data for downstream workflows.

## Differentiation

The product runs as a mobile web app, so workers can use a phone or tablet browser without installing a native app.

## Mission Statement

Help warehouse workers capture product-label data faster by turning mobile camera scans into structured OCR results that can be reviewed, stored, and exported directly from the browser.
